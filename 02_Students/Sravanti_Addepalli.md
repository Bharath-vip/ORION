# Dr. Sravanti Addepalli

**Role:** Alumni (PhD 2018-2024), now Research Scientist at Google DeepMind
**Lab:** Vision and AI Lab (VAL), IISc
**Advisor:** Prof. R. Venkatesh Babu

## Research Topic
Robustness in deep learning, adversarial machine learning, and vision-language models. Her research ensures that neural networks can defend against adversarial attacks and maintain reliability.

## Datasets Used
Adversarial robustness benchmarks (e.g., standard CIFAR-10/100, ImageNet, and their adversarially perturbed variants) alongside vision-language multimodal datasets.

## Models Built
Deep Neural Networks with fortified architectures for adversarial defense, robust Vision-Language Models, and specialized training pipelines designed to withstand adversarial perturbations.

## Most Cited / Key Papers
She is the recipient of the **IKDD Doctoral Dissertation Award (2024)**, **IUPRAI Doctoral Dissertation Award (2024)**, and the **Sir Vithal N. Chandavarkar Memorial Medal**. Her highly cited works concentrate on adversarial machine learning and creating provably robust deep neural network architectures.
