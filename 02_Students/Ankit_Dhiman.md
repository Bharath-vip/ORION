# Ankit Dhiman

**Role:** PhD Student (ERP from Samsung R&D, Joined Aug 2021)
**Lab:** Vision and AI Lab (VAL), IISc
**Advisor:** Prof. R. Venkatesh Babu

## Research Topic
3D computer vision, 3D reconstruction, novel-view synthesis, and generative models. His work pushes the boundaries of 3D Gaussian Splatting and 3D instance segmentation.

## Datasets Used
Standard 3D vision and novel-view synthesis datasets (e.g., Mip-NeRF 360, Tanks & Temples, ScanNet), as well as reflection and mirror-centric datasets.

## Models Built
3D Gaussian Splatting architectures, Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs), 3D instance segmentation networks, and Diffusion models tailored for specific visual phenomena (like mirror reflection generation).

## Most Cited / Key Papers
- **"Turbo-GS"** and **"AdaptiveSplat"** (3D Gaussian Splatting)
- **"Strata-NeRF"** (Novel-view synthesis)
- **"UniC-Lift"** (3D instance segmentation)
- **"MirrorFusion / MirrorVerse"** (Diffusion models for mirror reflections)
