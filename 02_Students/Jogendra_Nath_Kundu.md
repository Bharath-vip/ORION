# Dr. Jogendra Nath Kundu

**Role:** Alumni (PhD), now Research Scientist at Reality Labs Research (Meta)
**Lab:** Vision and AI Lab (VAL), IISc
**Advisor:** Prof. R. Venkatesh Babu

## Research Topic
Self-supervised domain adaptation frameworks, debiasing diffusion models, and 3D monocular object detection.

## Datasets Used
Domain adaptation datasets, 3D detection benchmarks (e.g., KITTI, nuScenes), and self-supervised learning datasets.

## Models Built
Domain adaptation frameworks, Diffusion models (specifically techniques for debiasing them), and 3D object detection models adapted from 2D visual cues.

## Most Cited / Key Papers
His thesis earned him the **IUPRAI Doctoral Dissertation Award (2023)** and an Honorable Mention for the **ACM India 2023 Doctoral Dissertation Award**. His highly cited papers focus on universal and source-free domain adaptation, 3D pose estimation, and self-supervised visual learning architectures.
