# Harsh Rangwani

**Role:** Alumni (PhD 2019-2024), now Research Scientist at Adobe Research
**Lab:** Vision and AI Lab (VAL), IISc
**Advisor:** Prof. R. Venkatesh Babu

## Research Topic
Learning from limited and imperfect data, long-tail learning, domain adaptation, optimization, and generative models. He focuses on algorithms that train effectively on real-world datasets with class imbalances and distribution shifts.

## Datasets Used
Imbalanced and shifted image datasets, such as ImageNet-LT, CIFAR-100-LT, and various domain adaptation benchmarks (e.g., Office-Home, VisDA).

## Models Built
Deep learning classification architectures, domain adaptation frameworks, and generative models optimized to handle highly skewed or imperfect data distributions.

## Most Cited / Key Papers
Winner of the **2024 IKDD Doctoral Dissertation in Data Science Award** and **IUPRAI Doctoral Dissertation Award 2024** for his thesis: *"Learning from Limited and Imperfect Data"*. He has multiple highly cited papers in top conferences (CVPR, ICCV) addressing long-tailed recognition and robust optimization.
