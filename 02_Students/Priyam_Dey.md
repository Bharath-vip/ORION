# Priyam Dey

**Role:** PhD Student
**Lab:** Vision and AI Lab (VAL), IISc
**Advisor:** Prof. R. Venkatesh Babu

## Research Topic
Computer vision and machine learning, with a strong focus on dataset distillation, generative models, and improving the generalizability and robustness of AI systems.

## Datasets Used
Primarily standard vision datasets suitable for dataset distillation and generative modeling tasks (e.g., CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, TinyImageNet, and subsets of ImageNet).

## Models Built
He works with Diffusion models (focusing on controllable erasure of concepts), ResNet architectures for distillation, and various neural networks designed for improved generalization (via Diversify-Aggregate-Repeat Training).

## Most Cited / Key Papers
- **"Rethinking Dataset Distillation: Hard Truths about Soft Labels"** (CVPR 2024, Oral presentation & Best Paper Finalist)
- **"Concept Siever: Towards Controllable Erasure of Concepts from Diffusion Models without Side-effects"** (TMLR 2024)
- **"DART: Diversify-Aggregate-Repeat Training Improves Generalization of Neural Networks"** (CVPR 2023)
