# Rishubh Parihar

**Role:** PhD Scholar (Joined 2021)
**Lab:** Vision and AI Lab (VAL), IISc
**Advisor:** Prof. R. Venkatesh Babu

## Research Topic
Controllable generative models, deep learning, image editing, and fairness/debiasing in vision-language models. He develops methods to enhance controllability in vision-based generative models by creating interfaces beyond text for model interaction.

## Datasets Used
Large-scale image and multimodal datasets used for image generation and zero-shot depth-aware image editing (e.g., COCO, CelebA-HQ, FFHQ, and custom fairness benchmarks).

## Models Built
Vision-based Generative Models, Diffusion models, and Vision-Language Models (VLMs) optimized for zero-shot editing, object placement, and reduced bias.

## Most Cited / Key Papers
He is a recipient of the Prof. Satish Dhawan Research Award (2024). Key works involve zero-shot depth-aware image editing, controllable generative editing mechanisms, and debiasing VLMs.
