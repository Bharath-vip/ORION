# Abhipsa Basu

**Role:** PhD Student (Joined Jan 2021)
**Lab:** Vision and AI Lab (VAL), IISc
**Advisor:** Prof. R. Venkatesh Babu

## Research Topic
Bias and fairness in AI-generative models, geographical representativeness in text-to-image models, and Visual Question Answering (VQA). She develops methods like "distribution guidance" to ensure fairer image generation without requiring model retraining.

## Datasets Used
She uses and curates diverse geographical datasets (e.g., the GeoDiv framework datasets) to evaluate cultural and geographical representation, alongside standard VQA datasets.

## Models Built
Text-to-Image Generative models, VQA models, and frameworks that modulate diffusion guidance to correct cultural and language biases dynamically.

## Most Cited / Key Papers
- Works proposing the **GeoDiv** framework for evaluating and improving the geographical diversity of text-to-image models.
- Papers focused on **distribution guidance** to mitigate bias in generative priors.
- Approaches to address language biases in VQA models.
