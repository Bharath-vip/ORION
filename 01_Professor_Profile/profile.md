# Prof. R. Venkatesh Babu (IISc)

## Overview
**Prof. R. Venkatesh Babu** is a distinguished Professor and the Chair of the Department of Computational and Data Sciences (CDS) at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore. He heads the **Vision and AI Lab (VAL)** at IISc and is recognized as one of the top AI researchers in India. His research significantly advances the fields of Computer Vision, Deep Learning, Image Processing, and Machine Learning.

## Academic History & Early Career
*   **Ph.D. (1999–2003):** Earned his Ph.D. from the Department of Electrical Engineering at IISc.
*   **Postdoctoral Research:** Conducted postdoctoral research at NTNU (Norway) and IRISA/INRIA (France) under an ERCIM fellowship.
*   **Early Career:** Served as a research fellow at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore, and worked in industry as a researcher at Yahoo Labs (2007–2008).
*   **IISc Faculty (2010–Present):** Joined IISc in August 2010 as an Assistant Professor, progressed to Associate Professor in 2016, and Professor in 2020. He assumed the role of Chair of CDS on January 1, 2024.

## Current Roles & Recognitions
*   **Leadership:** Chair of the Department of Computational and Data Sciences (CDS) at IISc, and Lead of the Vision and AI Lab (VAL).
*   **Fellowships:** Inducted as a Fellow of the Indian National Academy of Engineering (INAE) in 2025. He is also a Senior Member of IEEE and AAAI, and a Distinguished Member of the ACM.
*   **Awards:**
    *   Prof. Rustum Choksi Award for Excellence in Research in Engineering (2025).
    *   SERB STAR Award (2020).
    *   Satish Dhawan Young Engineer Award (2019).
*   **Rankings:** Featured consistently in the Stanford/Elsevier Top 2% Scientists List. CSRankings highlights him as a top AI researcher in India, and VAL as a leading AI lab in the country.

## Core Research Areas
Prof. Babu's research bridges theoretical advancements in deep learning with practical applications. Key areas include:
*   **Dataset Distillation & Data Efficiency:** Developing techniques for efficient deep neural network training (e.g., dataset condensation).
*   **Machine Unlearning & Privacy:** Researching data privacy, machine learning security, fairness, and bias mitigation (e.g., dataset membership verification).
*   **Adversarial Robustness & Defense:** Studying adversarial attacks and mechanisms like adversarial training to make AI models resilient.
*   **Generative AI & 3D Vision:** Exploring diffusion models, 3D-aware computer vision (e.g., NeRFs, Gaussian Splatting), and generative models.
